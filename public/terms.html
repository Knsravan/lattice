What this site is

Lattice is a free, educational explanation of lattice-based post-quantum cryptography. It is made for learning, not for protecting real data.

Not for real security

The Kyber, LWE and attack demos use tiny toy numbers so every step fits on screen. They are not secure. Never use them, or any value they produce, to protect real messages, passwords or keys. For real use, rely on audited libraries that implement ML-KEM (FIPS 203).
